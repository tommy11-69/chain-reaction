    STEP 02 MAIN SECRET

    Critical Mass (Vedikkira Limit 💥)

    Board-la irukura ovvoru cell-kum oru "Thangura Limit" (Critical Mass) irukku. Andha cell-ku ethana pakkatthu neighbors (borders) irukko, adhudhaan adhodha limit! Limit vandhuduchuna... PADAAR-NU VEDIKKUM!

    MOOLA (CORNER) LIMIT: 2 ORBS

    2 borders dhaan irukku. 2nd orb vecha udane left/right/top/down-la 2 pakkamum sithari vedichidum!

    VARAIMBU (EDGE) LIMIT: 3 ORBS

    3 borders irukku. 3rd orb vandhuduchuna moonu direction-layum blast aagi orbs split aagidum!

    NADU CELL (CENTER) LIMIT: 4 ORBS

    4 borders irukku (Mela, Keela, Left, Right). 4th orb vandha naalu pakkamum nuclear blast!

    BONUS THALA LEVEL HACKS

    Noobie To Pro: Semma Tips 🧠

    TIP #1

    Corner dhaan Raja 👑

    Corner cells-la 2 orbs-laye blast aagidum. Early game-la eppovum corner-a cover pannu. Romba safe and offensive.

    TIP #2

    Vera aaloda blast zone-la poi sikkadha! 🚫

    Ethiri corner-la 1 orb vechu ready-a irundha, pakkathu cell-la poi orb-a vekkaadha. Avan 2nd orb vecha unnayum sethu thookiduvaan.

    TIP #3

    Critical Mass-1 Stage-la Load Panni Vai 🔋

    Cells-a blast aagura boundary-la (Corner-la 1, Edge-la 2, Center-la 3) load panni vainga. Right opportunity kedaikkum bodhu ore oru orb podu... motha rajyamum unnodadhu!

    TIP #4

    8-Player Chaos Mode Alert! 🚨

    8 peru aadrappo board bayangarama congested-a irukkum. Oru chinna blast kooda tsunami maari suthura ellarayum wipeout pannidum!
